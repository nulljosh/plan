A+ in a year.

LECSS, KPU, SFU, UBC

Calculus, physics, programming — then engineering.

Plan Sans is Inter v4.1, an open font cut after San Francisco, renamed and tuned for this page. Round quotes and commas (“like this,”) and the tailed l (Illinois, 1lI) are baked in. Optical size follows the font size, so headlines get the tighter display cut and body text the open text cut, the way SF Display and SF Text split. Weights 100–900, Latin only.

Proportional1,111 · $1,740 · 2028 Tabular1,111 · $1,740 · 2028

Figures are proportional by default, so numbers in running text sit evenly. Tables and the chart switch to tabular so columns line up. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License; see OFL.txt.

Rules

Keep it

  • One accent. Plan blue for what's live; everything else is ink on paper.
  • Square corners. 2px on buttons, cards and tiles; only the app icon rounds.
  • Caps for labels only. Headings stay sentence case.
  • Numbers are cited. A figure on the plan has a source in the footer drawer.
  • No inline styles on the plan page; new pieces go in plan.css and show up here.
