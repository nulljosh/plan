School and path

SFU Computing Science — a real Bachelor of Science in Burnaby, easier admission than UBC. Degree, not diploma. Calc and physics in year 1 so engineering stays open, with a Business minor at SFU Beedie (or an MSc Finance after).

Then UBC — engineering and graduate work, research, and the long game: a faculty position, and faculty housing on the Endowment Lands.

Ruled out as the degree: UVic (not Lower Mainland) · KPU (no real CS degree — the launchpad instead) · BCIT (too vocational, closes the engineering door).

Finance trackSFU Beedie

CS is the base. Finance is the multiplier — it's what turns a dev job into quant/fintech/trading pay. Two ways to add it, pick one at end of year 2:

Business minor Default

Beedie, ~10–11 courses inside the BSc

What it costs
Beedie has no Finance minor. The Business minor is BUS 200/251/311/340/341/401 + electives; load the electives with finance (BUS 312/315/316).
Best if
You want to graduate on time and work as a dev at a fintech/bank.

MSc Finance

Beedie, 16 months post-BSc

What it costs
~$41k domestic tuition, one extra year of no salary. Needs decent GPA + GMAT/GRE.
Best if
You want quant / portfolio / trading roles, not just engineering.

Default: take the minor. It's free relative to the degree and keeps the master's open — MSc Finance admits CS grads, and a Business minor + CS BSc is a stronger application than CS alone. Decide on the master's after you've worked 1–2 years and know whether you actually want the quant side.

Practical note: keep first-year calc and stats strong — both the minor and MSc lean on them, and it's the same calc you're already taking to keep engineering open. No extra prereq cost.

Money

Tuition and grants

  • KPU, Spring 2027: 15 credits of regular tuition, no extra course fees. StudentAid BC and family.
  • SFU: ~$7,000–7,500/yr ($220.61/unit × 30 units + fees, 2026–27). Living at home saves the big cost, housing.
  • CSG-D: up to $2,800/yr for tuition and books, via StudentAid BC. CSG-DSE: up to $20,000/yr for equipment and tutoring, via the school's accessibility office. PWD medical evidence speeds up verification.

Also worth having

  • GST/HST Credit — automatic once you file taxes, keep filing every year.
  • CWB disability supplement — kicks in once you have co-op/part-time income.
  • Community Living BC (CLBC) — supports for adults with autism diagnosis, worth a conversation.
  • PWD health supplements — dental, equipment, some therapy costs. Ask your worker what's covered.
